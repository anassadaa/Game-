# Touchline 2.5 — Club investments and ownership

Android version 2.5, build 16. Updates the existing com.anassadaa.touchline app with the original signing identity.

## New features
- Invest personal money in other clubs through Club → Investments. Browse a searchable market, track a portfolio, and open a separate office for each controlled club.
- Independent club cash, operating ledgers, bank debt, managers, spending limits, infrastructure and financial history. Share purchases pay selling shareholders; loans and new equity fund the club.
- Adaptive equity valuations combine operating earnings, revenue, cash, debt, squad quality, facilities, league status and results. Price history and explanations show why estimates change.
- Whole-percentage share trading with explicit fees, finite sale liquidity, willing sellers and a disclosed takeover premium. Quotes must still match the current state when confirmed.
- 51% unlocks majority decisions. 50% requires agreement; 25–50% shareholders can submit contextual proposals. Selling or accepting outside equity can remove control.
- Hire or dismiss managers; choose development strategy, delegation, transfer and wage limits, ticket prices, commercial strategy and dividend policy.
- Build academy, scouting and stadium projects; review delegated transfer requests; appoint managers to run other clubs while keeping one personal football job.
- Shareholder loans and repayment, new equity, bank borrowing and repayment, outside investment and dilution. Financing is excluded from earned profit; dividends come from profit and available reserves.
- Separate personal cash, estimated equity, repayable loan claims, investment cost, realized returns and career dividends.
- Existing saves migrate without resetting wallets or holdings. Legacy purchase cost bases start at migration valuation because old saves did not record historical prices.

## Integration repairs
- Existing recruitment and sales now obey investment spending limits and settle against the appropriate club ledger. Instalment principal excludes agent fees and is paid exactly once.
- Funding actions synchronize unbooked expenses before changing the ledger. Owners who personally coach their club retain dividend entitlement; repeat distributions for one season are blocked.
- Job changes preserve independent cash, facilities, construction and ticket policies. Hostile votes no longer force a majority shareholder to sell in the investment system.
- Incumbent manager ability is measured against its opening baseline so existing football balance is preserved.
- Financial import validation rejects malformed market data. Portrait and landscape investment layouts include confirmation dialogs and control-loss warnings.

## Design references and boundaries
The game uses one vote per whole percentage of equity, with 51% as a clear gameplay control threshold. This is a simplification: ordinary resolutions use a simple majority, while some real company changes require larger majorities ([Companies Act s282](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/46/section/282), [Companies House guidance](https://www.gov.uk/make-changes-to-your-limited-company/get-agreement-from-your-company)).

Equity estimates add cash and subtract debt from the operating value, rather than treating new funding as operating earnings ([CFA Institute valuation overview](https://www.cfainstitute.org/insights/professional-learning/refresher-readings/2026/market-based-valuation-price-enterprise-value-multiples)). Formula weights, transaction fees and takeover premiums are game-balancing choices, not live market prices.

Real multi-club ownership can be restricted by competition rules ([UEFA Article 5](https://documents.uefa.com/r/Regulations-of-the-UEFA-Conference-League-2025/26/Article-5-Integrity-of-the-competition/multi-club-ownership-Online)). This release implements simulated ownership and does not model the full UEFA admission framework.

Signing credentials remain outside the repository and are supplied only by the existing CI secrets.
