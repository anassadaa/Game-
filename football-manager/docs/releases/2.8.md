# Touchline 2.8

1. What was broken and is now fixed
   - Nothing new was broken in the money, ownership or transfer rules. Those stay as fixed in 2.6 and 2.7.
   - The match picture was a flat diagram. It is now a floodlit 3D view that follows the ball. This is the one exception you allowed to the “no 3D” rule.

2. What was redesigned
   - Match day. Main camera stays on the ball. Touchline, Behind goal and Tactical are the other angles.
   - Players have a kit, a number, a face and a run. The ball has panels and a ring on the grass so you can see it.
   - Speed buttons and “Sim to result” are still there.

3. New fun
   - You can watch the move, including a goal from behind the net, instead of only reading the text.
   - The crowd noise swells when the ball is in the box. Tap the picture once if you want the sound.

4. Not done, and why
   - A brand-new deadline-day drama, extra dressing-room events and a new club-history page were not added. Shouts, derbies, the inbox and the trophy celebrations already cover a lot of that, and this release was the match picture.
   - Fresh screenshots from a realme GT Neo 3 were not saved into the repo. Please look at match day on the phone after you install 2.8 and say if anything is cut off.

5. Tests
   - 292 tests passed. Typecheck was clean.
