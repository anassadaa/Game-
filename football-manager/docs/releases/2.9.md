# Touchline 2.9 — the manager runs the football

In Director, Owner-Director and Chairman careers your hired manager now does everything you do in Manager mode:

- **Team and tactics:** picks the XI and bench every match by position, fitness and sharpness; better managers rest tired players sooner; switches mentality after a bad run and drifts back to his own style.
- **Staff:** hires coaches/physio/assistant for empty roles within the staff budget, replaces weak staff when a clearly better one is available, renews good staff late in the season.
- **Contracts:** renews key players with 1 year or less left if the wage budget allows; lets old or weak ones run down (logged).
- **Transfers:** in windows, finds his weakest detailed position (GK, LB, CB, RB, DM, CM, AM, LW, RW, ST), buys within your transfer budget and wage budget, sells surplus older/weak players. Deals over your approval line come to you to approve or block (unanswered requests expire after 2 weeks).
- **Board screen → First-team manager:** W-D-L, league position vs expected, budget used/left, edit transfer budget, wage budget and approval line, approve/block, plain-English decision log.
- Every 4 weeks his position vs expectations moves board trust and fan mood.

Manager mode, the match engine and the 3D match are unchanged. All decisions are hash-based and deterministic.
