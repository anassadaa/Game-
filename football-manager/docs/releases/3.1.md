# Touchline 3.1

## Fixed
- **The hired manager no longer vanishes.** The season-end board review and the mid-season low-trust check were quietly sacking him in Owner/Chairman mode. He now stays until his contract ends unless he quits or is poached, which is rare (never in his first season), always announced in a headline, and leaves a caretaker in charge.
- **Release fees are realistic.** If he leaves early, the club gets a release fee booked as income: about a quarter of his remaining wages, never more than half a season of his wages.
- **No more negative budget.** The manager's budgets come from real cash after the weekly running costs, his own salary and the staff wages. If the club goes into the red, the board freezes signings and forces sales. Below zero, the budget shows "Overspent £X".
- **No more staff churn.** The manager makes at most one staff change per window and never pays off staff when the bank is negative. Empty roles get refilled.
- Ledger season label, dividends skipped every other season, forced shareholder sale paying nothing, free-agent and loanee flipping: all fixed. See docs/review-3.1.md.

## New
- **Finance tab** in the bottom nav with bank, budgets (including the manager's automatic budget and your %), income against expenses by category, spending limit and release clauses, manager wages, compensation and fees received, and your wallet.
